Ecosystem Balance
Every animal plays a critical role in maintaining the delicate balance of our planet's ecosystems.
Predators control prey populations to prevent overgrazing, while herbivores help spread plant seeds across vast distances.
Insects like bees and butterflies pollinate flowers, ensuring the growth of crops and wild plants that produce oxygen.
Even decomposers and scavengers like vultures and beetles perform vital work by cleaning up waste and recycling nutrients
back into the soil.
