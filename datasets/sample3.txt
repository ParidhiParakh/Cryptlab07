Diverse Habitats
Creatues have adapted to live in almost every environment on Earth, from the freezing polar regions to scorching deserts.
Marine animals like fish, whales, and corals thrive in deep oceans, while amphibians and reptiles often prefer wetlands 
and tropical rainforests. Birds have mastered the skies, and mammals occupy diverse terrains ranging from open grasslands
to dense underground burrows. These distinct habitats shape how animals hunt, communicate, and protect themselves from
predators.
