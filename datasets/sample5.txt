Human Connection
Humans share a deep, historical connection with the animal world that influences our daily lives.
We rely on domesticated species for food, clothing, and agricultural labour, while companion animals like dogs and cats
offer emotional support and loyalty. Beyond practical uses, wildlife inspires art, scientific innovation, and global tourism,
reminding us of our responsibility to protect endangered species. Preserving animal habitats is ultimately essential for
ensuring the long-term survival of humanity itself.
